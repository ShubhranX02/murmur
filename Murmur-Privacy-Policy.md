## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information. We do not collect any information beyond what is described here, and we do not use your data for any purpose other than those stated in Section 3.

### 2.1 Information from Google Sign-In
When you sign in with Google, we receive:
- Your name
- Your email address
- Your profile photo

### 2.2 Information from YouTube (via the YouTube Data API, `youtube.readonly` scope)
With your explicit permission, granted through Google's consent screen, we access:
- Your liked videos (title, description/tags, category, channel name, thumbnail) — up to your 200 most recent likes
- Your channel subscriptions (up to 200)

We use this data to generate a **taste profile**. The taste profile consists of:
- A numeric "embedding" vector representing the content of your most recently liked videos (calculated from your 50 most recent likes)
- A breakdown of your interests by YouTube content category

**We do not store your raw liked-video list or subscription list as a permanently browsable history feature.** The retained outputs are the embedding vector, the category breakdown, and summary counts (see Section 2.5).

### 2.3 Information you provide directly
- City (selected from our fixed list of eligible Indian locations)
- Age
- Gender (Male, Female, or Other)
- An optional profile description (up to 100 words)

### 2.4 Information generated by using the Service
- Messages you send in direct chats, group chats, and public conversation rooms
- Which conversation rooms you create, join, or leave
- Match scores computed between you and other members
- Read/unread status of your conversations

### 2.5 Aggregate and derived data shown to other members
Your public Murmur profile shows: your name, photo, city, age, gender, optional description, your total analyzed liked-video count, the number of content categories detected, and a chart of your category breakdown. It also shows your match score with the member viewing it.

**We never show other members:** your email address, your raw liked-video list, your subscription list, or your embedding vector. These remain private to you and to Murmur's matching system.

### 2.6 Technical and log data
Standard technical data generated by using a web application, such as IP address, browser/device type, and timestamps of requests, may be captured by our infrastructure providers for security and operational purposes.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

| Data | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Google profile (name, email, photo) | Account creation, sign-in, display identity |
| Liked videos & subscriptions | One-time (and periodic, at your choice) computation of your taste-profile embedding and category breakdown |
| Embedding & category breakdown | Calculating compatibility ("match") scores with other members |
| City & age | Prioritizing candidates who are geographically and generationally closer to you, before compatibility scoring |
| Description | Displayed on your profile to other members |
| Chat messages | Delivering conversations between you and your matches or conversation-room participants |
| Technical/log data | Security, fraud prevention, debugging, and service reliability |

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## 4. Your Consent and How to Withdraw It

## 6. Data Retention

- **Account and profile data** is retained for as long as your account is active.
- **Taste-profile data** (embedding, category breakdown) is refreshed approximately every 7 days if you reconnect YouTube, or retained as last computed if you choose to continue with your saved profile.
- **Chat and conversation-room messages** are retained for as long as the relevant chat or room exists, or as described at account deletion below.
- **Upon account deletion**, we delete or irreversibly anonymize your profile, taste-profile data, and matching records within 30 days. Chat messages you sent as part of a shared conversation with another member may be retained remaining in the shared conversation history (but without your profile identity), because deleting your account does not delete messages held in another member's chat record.
